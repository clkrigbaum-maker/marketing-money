# Top 5 No-Drill Security Cameras for Your Apartment in 2025

## Introduction

Navigating the world of home security can be daunting, especially when you live in an apartment. You need a solution that is effective, easy to install, and, most importantly, doesn't require any drilling or permanent modifications. Fortunately, the market for no-drill security cameras has exploded, offering a wealth of options that are perfect for renters. In this post, we'll showcase our top five picks for no-drill security cameras in 2025, highlighting their key features, installation methods, and why they are ideal for apartment living.

## 1. Blink Outdoor 4: The Versatile Workhorse

The Blink Outdoor 4 is a perennial favorite for a reason. It's a wire-free, battery-powered camera that offers exceptional flexibility and performance. Its long battery life (up to two years on a single set of AA lithium batteries) means you can set it and forget it. The camera delivers crisp 1080p HD video and includes features like two-way audio, customizable motion detection, and person detection (with a Blink subscription).

**No-Drill Installation:** The Blink Outdoor 4 comes with a simple mounting kit that can be attached to surfaces using adhesive strips. For outdoor use on a balcony or patio, you can use no-drill gutter mounts or vinyl siding clips, which are widely available on Amazon. This makes it incredibly easy to place the camera wherever you need it without any permanent damage.

**Why it's great for apartments:** Its wire-free design and versatile mounting options make it perfect for both indoor and outdoor use in a rental setting. You can easily monitor your front door, balcony, or living room without running any cables or drilling any holes.

## 2. Eufy SoloCam S340: The Solar-Powered Sentinel

The Eufy SoloCam S340 is a powerhouse of a camera, offering a unique dual-camera system (a wide-angle and a telephoto lens) that provides both a broad overview and detailed zoom capabilities. What truly sets it apart is its integrated solar panel, which provides continuous power, eliminating the need to ever change batteries. It also boasts 360-degree pan and tilt, AI-powered tracking, and local storage with no monthly fees.

**No-Drill Installation:** The SoloCam S340 can be mounted using no-drill methods such as pole mounts or adhesive mounting brackets. Its solar panel requires a few hours of direct sunlight per day, so placement is key, but its wire-free design means you have plenty of flexibility.

**Why it's great for apartments:** The solar charging and local storage make it a very low-maintenance and cost-effective option. The pan-and-tilt functionality allows you to cover a wide area, such as a large living room or a balcony with multiple entry points, with just a single camera.

## 3. Wyze Cam OG: The Budget-Friendly Champion

For those on a tight budget, the Wyze Cam OG is an unbeatable choice. This compact, indoor/outdoor camera offers 1080p color night vision, motion detection, and a built-in spotlight, all for an incredibly low price. It's a plug-in camera, so you'll need to be near an outlet, but its performance and features are impressive for its price point.

**No-Drill Installation:** The Wyze Cam OG has a magnetic base and comes with a mounting plate that can be attached with adhesive. This makes it easy to stick on a wall, ceiling, or any magnetic surface. Its small size also means it can be discreetly placed on a shelf or bookshelf.

**Why it's great for apartments:** It's the perfect entry-level camera for monitoring the inside of your apartment. Its low cost means you can get multiple cameras to cover different rooms without breaking the bank. The spotlight and color night vision are also great features for deterring potential intruders.

## 4. Ring Peephole Cam: The Front Door Guardian

The Ring Peephole Cam is a brilliant solution for apartment dwellers who want to monitor their front door. It replaces your existing peephole, so there's no drilling or permanent modification required. It offers 1080p video, two-way talk, and knock detection, and it's battery-powered, making it completely wire-free.

**No-Drill Installation:** Installation is as simple as unscrewing your existing peephole and replacing it with the Peephole Cam. It's a completely reversible process that won't damage your door.

**Why it's great for apartments:** It's the most discreet and landlord-friendly way to add a video doorbell to your apartment. You get all the benefits of a video doorbell without any of the installation hassles. It's perfect for seeing who's at your door, whether you're home or away.

If you're looking for top-of-the-line performance, the Arlo Pro 5S 2K is a fantastic choice. It offers stunning 2K HDR video, color night vision, a 160-degree field of view, and an integrated spotlight. It's a wire-free, battery-powered camera with a magnetic charging cable for easy recharging.
